Advisor: **Xuming Zhang**.

I built a three-stage registration pipeline combining **sparse Gaussian deformation**, **multi-scale adaptive density control**, and **NCC/TVD-regularized optimization**.

Gradient-guided densification and pruning improve fine-detail regions. Structural segmentation emphasizes organ boundaries and functional regions without requiring large-scale pre-annotations.
