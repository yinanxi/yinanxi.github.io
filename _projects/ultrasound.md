At **Wuhan United Imaging Healthcare**, I studied imitation and diffusion policies, world-model navigation, hierarchical LLM/VLM planning, and end-to-end vision-language-action approaches. I proposed a staged development roadmap built around synchronized image, pose, force, and language data.

- Benchmarked **Qwen2-VL-7B** on **300 U2-BENCH cases**: 40.0% accuracy against a 34.4% random baseline.
- Reproduced **OpenVLA** on **30 LIBERO episodes**: 86.7% success.
- Validated **LoRA adaptation** by training 1.45% of 7.65B parameters on one A100 GPU.
- Built a **PyBullet/KUKA** simulator mapping probe poses to synthetic B-mode slices in a closed loop.
- Analyzed temporal RGB-D/IMU fusion for probe-state estimation using sequence encoders and cross-attention.
