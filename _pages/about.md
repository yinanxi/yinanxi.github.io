I am an undergraduate at **Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)**, pursuing a B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering and a second bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology, with graduation expected in June 2027.

At **MAIR Lab, HUST**, I am fortunate to work with [Prof. Zhiyuan Ma](https://ponymzy.github.io/) on diffusion-model alignment, while leading the development of the OJ Tutor component of [EduNexus](/projects/edunexus/). In summer 2025, I was fortunate to be a visiting student at the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, North Carolina State University](https://ece.ncsu.edu/). In summer 2026, I joined [United Imaging Healthcare](https://www.united-imaging.com/en/) as a research intern, where I worked on an autonomous ultrasound scanning robot.

Beyond research, I enjoy tennis 🎾, traveling 🌌, debating 🎙️, piano 🎹, and nature photography 📷. I am always excited to meet curious people and explore new collaborations, so feel free to email me with a research idea—or simply to say hello.

I dream of traveling through space, but if that dream remains beyond my reach, I hope to see my robots carry our imagination with them—helping us explore and understand our world for the betterment of humanity.

### Research interests

- **World models & generative modeling:** diffusion and flow models, reward guidance and alignment, multimodal generation.
- **Generative policies & physical AI:** vision-language-action models, diffusion policies, embodied agents, spatial intelligence, and 3D perception.
- **Generative modeling for biology:** protein and DNA generation, variable-length generation, and autonomous laboratories for drug discovery.
