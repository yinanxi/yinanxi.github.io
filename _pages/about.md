I am an undergraduate at **Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)**, pursuing a B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering and a second bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology, with graduation expected in June 2027.

My research interests center on **generative modeling and intelligent agents**: how to learn useful models of the world, align generative models with rewards, and turn generation into action. I am also interested in bringing these methods to biological discovery.

At **MAIR Lab, HUST**, I work on diffusion-model alignment with **Zhiyuan Ma** and **Bowen Zhou**, and lead the development of the OJ Tutor component of **EduNexus**. My experience also includes autonomous ultrasound scanning at **United Imaging Healthcare** and summer research with **Bowen Xu** at **NC State University**.

[Email](mailto:yinanxi010502@gmail.com) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Y3Cx1I0AAAAJ&hl=en) · [GitHub](https://github.com/yinanxi) · [CV](/cv/)

### Research interests

- **World models & generative modeling:** diffusion and flow models, reward guidance and alignment, multimodal generation.
- **Generative policies & physical AI:** vision-language-action models, diffusion policies, embodied agents, spatial intelligence, and 3D perception.
- **Generative modeling for biology:** protein and DNA generation, variable-length generation, and autonomous laboratories for drug discovery.
